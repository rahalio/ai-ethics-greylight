# Greylight — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### AI product manager and feature owner

- As a feature owner, I want the gate to tell me at use case definition which concerns my idea raises before I commit an engineering team, so that I discover the objection at the cheapest possible moment.
- As a feature owner, I want a clear verdict of proceed, deliberate or barred rather than a list of open questions, so that I can plan the release instead of waiting for consensus to form.
- As a feature owner, I want to see whether a similar dilemma has already been resolved for my market, so that I inherit a position rather than re-opening an argument the organisation has already had.
- As a feature owner, I want to record a decision not to build with the reasoning attached, so that the six months of work we chose not to do is visible as a deliberate call rather than as a gap in the roadmap.

### Data scientist and ML engineer

- As a data scientist, I want the data-stage review to ask specifically whether our collected data marginalises particular demographics, so that the question is answered with evidence rather than left to my judgement.
- As an ML engineer, I want to record what private or unintended information could be inferred from our predictions, so that a foreseeable inference risk is a design constraint instead of a post-launch surprise.
- As a data scientist, I want to challenge a breach check I believe is misapplied to my system, so that a bright line drawn for the wrong reason can be corrected rather than quietly circumvented. *(exception path)*

### AI risk leader

- As the AI risk leader, I want the dilemma docket ordered by launch date and severity, so that the forum's limited time is spent on the questions that are about to become irreversible.
- As the AI risk leader, I want dissent recorded when the forum does not reach agreement, so that a close call is documented as a close call and can be revisited when circumstances change.
- As the AI risk leader, I want alerting when a resolved position is being applied outside the context it was decided under, so that a Canadian answer is not silently exported to a market with different social norms.
- As the AI risk leader, I want every open concern to show its named owner, so that I can escalate to a person rather than to a team.

### Deliberation forum member and employee representative

- As a forum member, I want the submission to state the anticipated harm and the affected group in the team's own words, so that I am deliberating on the real question rather than on a sanitised summary.
- As an employee representative, I want to record the workforce's anticipated response to an automation initiative before deployment is approved, so that the people whose work changes are consulted rather than informed.
- As a forum member, I want to attach conditions to a proceed decision, so that we can permit a use case on terms rather than choosing between blanket approval and blanket refusal.

### Executive sponsor and platform administrator

- As an executive sponsor, I want exposure reported across regulatory conflict, public backlash and operational inefficiency with a named owner for each, so that I am governing three distinct problems rather than one undifferentiated risk.
- As a platform administrator, I want to maintain the concern taxonomy and the forum's quorum rules, and to have every change to them versioned, so that a verdict reached last year can be interpreted against the rules that applied at the time. *(governance / admin story)*
- As an executive sponsor, I want every time-boxed breach acceptance listed with its expiry and my name against it, so that exceptions I granted come back to me instead of becoming permanent.
